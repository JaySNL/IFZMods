| Expanded Farming 72 | Farm sizes were lost on reload when the save was reloaded on a later in-game day than the farms were placed (sidecar save-key drifted — it was keyed on the save name, which embeds the in-game clock) | High | 🟢 Fixed | Shipped **0.1.2** — sidecar key now uses map coordinates (clock-independent), with a one-time auto-migration that recovers existing saves |
| Surrounded 55 | Lairs bred to capacity but never launched a wave — the release trigger was 90% of *lair capacity*, and vanilla recomputes that capacity from the PLAYER's citizen count (`Hideout.RecalculateMainGroupCapacity`), so on a large late base capacity outgrew breeding and the 90% mark receded forever (day-109 report: 49 lairs, 3578 infected hoarded, zero waves all night) | High | 🟢 Fixed | Shipped **0.4.7** (2026-07-25) — fires at the capacity fraction OR an absolute `LairReleaseThreshold` body count, whichever comes first; new `LairWaveMaxRelease` (300) caps one wave so the unblocked pump can't dump thousands at once; Verbose now logs per-gate lair counts |

## How to use this page

- **Players** — found a bug or have a request? [Open an issue](https://github.com/JaySNL/IFZMods/issues/new/choose). Include your save day, OS (Windows / Proton / Mac), and which mods are installed.
- **Dev** — when a bug is reported, add a row to *Open bugs*; move to 🟢 Fixed (with the version) once shipped, then prune after a couple of cycles.
- Release-pipeline detail (BUILT → VERIFIED → PUSHED) lives in `tools/nexus-publish/RELEASE-QUEUE.md`; this page is the public-facing summary.
